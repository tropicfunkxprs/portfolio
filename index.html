🌞 Executive Summary (2023–2025)

Tropic Funkxprs is a Borneo-born live band based in Kuala Lumpur, founded by Khairul S. and Alfian Ahmad. Their signature sound fuses tropical funk, soul, and pop — uniting island rhythm with city groove.

From Burger King Malaysia’s annual dinners to Ford Raptor Rev 2025, L’Oréal Malaysia, BMW Motorrad, and Sime Motors, the band has become a trusted name in corporate, lifestyle, and festival entertainment. They’ve also made creative marks through YSDAF 2023, KKCD 2024, and multiple charity shows.
