Executive Summary

Tropic Funkxprs is a Borneo-born live band based in Kuala Lumpur, founded by Khairul S. and Alfian Ahmad. Their sound fuses tropical funk, soul and pop — trusted in corporate, lifestyle and festival entertainment across Malaysia. The band continues to represent modern Borneo energy: polished, soulful, and unforgettable.
