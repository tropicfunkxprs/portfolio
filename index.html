Executive Summary

Tropic Funkxprs is a Borneo-born live band based in Kuala Lumpur, founded by Khairul S. and Alfian Ahmad. Their sound fuses tropical funk, soul, and pop — uniting island rhythm with city groove. Trusted across corporate and festival stages including Burger King Malaysia, Ford Malaysia, L'Oréal Malaysia, BMW Motorrad and more.
